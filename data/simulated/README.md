# Simulated fleet batches (for the Fleet scoring demo)

Generated by `python -m pipelines.simulate_fleet` from `train_FD001` and `test_FD001` (+ `RUL_FD001`). Each file has 100 engines in the raw FD001 schema with a header row. Every engine is a real FD001 engine cut off at a chosen point, with small Gaussian sensor noise added.

* `batch1.csv`: healthy fleet: most engines still have plenty of life left (10017 rows; 43 engines from test, 57 from train; 0 engines truly at or past 80% of their life)
* `batch2.csv`: mixed fleet: engines spread across the whole life (11823 rows; 39 engines from test, 61 from train; 17 engines truly at or past 80% of their life)
* `batch3.csv`: ageing fleet: many engines close to the end of their life (15403 rows; 35 engines from test, 65 from train; 51 engines truly at or past 80% of their life)

`truth/batchN_truth.csv` holds each engine's true remaining life and source engine. The app never reads it unless you upload it as labels.

Engines derived from train engines may be ones the saved models were fitted on, so their predictions are optimistic. These are simulations of the same simulated fleet, not independent real data.
